# Follow-Up Prompts: Improving the Email

**Tool used:** Claude (Claude Sonnet 5.5, via Claude Code). Answers are pasted unedited.
**Starting point:** the email from the clear prompt in [vague-vs-clear.md](vague-vs-clear.md).

## Follow-up 1: More polite

> Make this email more polite and respectful, without making it much longer.

**Subject: Respectful Request to Submit Data Structures Assignment (Linked Lists)**

Dear Prof. Patil,

I hope you are doing well. I sincerely apologise that I was unable to submit the Data Structures assignment on linked lists by Monday's deadline. I had a fever and was resting for two days.

I have completed the assignment since then, and I can share a doctor's note if you would like to see it.

If you are able to allow it, I would be very grateful to submit the work by this Friday. Thank you very much for your time and understanding.

With respect,
Tejas Bhoite
[Roll No.]

**What changed:** added a greeting, "sincerely apologise", and softer wording ("if you are able to allow it", "very grateful").

## Follow-up 2: Shorter

> Now make it much shorter, under 70 words.

**Subject: Late Submission: Data Structures Assignment**

Dear Prof. Patil,

I missed Monday's linked list assignment deadline because of a fever and apologise. The work is complete, and I have a doctor's note. May I submit it by Friday?

Thank you,
Tejas Bhoite
[Roll No.]

**What changed:** cut it from about 90 words to about 40 by dropping the greeting phrases and extra detail, but the request and the new date stayed.

## Follow-up 3: As bullet points

> Rewrite the email as bullet points for the key details, with a one-line opening and closing.

**Subject: Late Submission Request: Data Structures Assignment**

Dear Prof. Patil,

I am writing about my missed assignment deadline. Key details:

- **Assignment:** Data Structures, linked lists (due Monday)
- **Reason:** fever, resting for two days
- **Status:** assignment is complete; doctor's note available
- **Request:** permission to submit by Friday

Thank you for your understanding.

Sincerely,
Tejas Bhoite
[Roll No.]

**What changed:** the paragraphs became four labelled points, so the professor can scan the facts quickly, but it feels less personal.

## My Notes

- Each follow-up changed only what I asked, and the facts (assignment, reason, Friday) stayed the same, which I checked each time.
- The shorter version was the most practical for a busy professor, but it lost the polite tone. For a first-time request I would send the polite version.
- Asking for one change at a time worked better than asking for everything in one prompt.
