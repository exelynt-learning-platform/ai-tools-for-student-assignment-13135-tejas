# Vague vs Clear Prompt: Email to a Professor About a Missed Deadline

**Tool used:** Claude (Claude Sonnet 5.5, via Claude Code). Both answers are pasted unedited.

## 1. Vague prompt

> Write an email to my professor about a missed deadline.

### Answer to the vague prompt

**Subject: Missed Deadline**

Dear Professor,

I am writing to inform you that I missed the deadline for the assignment. I apologize for the inconvenience. Please let me know if there is anything I can do to make up for it.

Thank you for your understanding.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

### What was wrong with it

The email is generic. It has no course name, no assignment, no reason, no new date, and the tone is unclear. I would have to rewrite most of it before sending, because the AI had nothing specific to work with.

## 2. Clear prompt (role, context, task, format)

> **Role:** You are a polite, professional writing assistant helping an engineering student.
> **Context:** I am Tejas Bhoite, a final-year Computer Science student. I missed the deadline for the Data Structures assignment on linked lists, which was due on Monday, because I had a fever and was resting for two days. I have the assignment finished now and have a doctor's note. My professor is Prof. Patil.
> **Task:** Write an email to Prof. Patil explaining why I missed the deadline, apologising once, and asking if I can submit the assignment by this Friday.
> **Format:** A subject line, then a formal email in 3 short paragraphs, under 150 words, ending with my name and roll number placeholder [Roll No.].

### Answer to the clear prompt

**Subject: Request to Submit Data Structures Assignment (Linked Lists) by Friday**

Dear Prof. Patil,

I am writing about the Data Structures assignment on linked lists, which was due on Monday. I missed the deadline because I had a fever and was resting for two days. I apologise for the delay.

The assignment is now complete. I have also kept a doctor's note, which I can show you if needed.

Would it be possible for me to submit it by this Friday? Thank you for your time and understanding.

Sincerely,
Tejas Bhoite
[Roll No.]

### What changed and my notes

- The clear version names the course, assignment, reason and a new date, so it is ready to send with one small edit (the roll number).
- Role told the AI what tone to use, context gave it the facts, task said what the email must do, and format fixed the length and structure.
- The vague answer was not wrong, just useless. The clear answer was only as good as the facts I gave it, so I still need to check that every detail in it is true before sending.
