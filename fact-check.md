# Fact Check: What Does AI Know About My College?

**Tool used:** ChatGPT
**College / University:** Rajarambapu Institute of Technology (RIT), Rajaramnagar, Sangli

## Prompt

> Answer these three questions about Rajarambapu Institute of Technology: 1) In which year was it established? 2) In which city is it located? 3) Which university is it affiliated with?

## Questions, AI answers and my check

| # | Question | AI's answer | Right / Wrong |
|---|----------|-------------|---------------|
| 1 | In which year was Rajarambapu Institute of Technology established? | It was established in 1983. | **Right.** The official website confirms the establishment year. |
| 2 | In which city is it located? | It is located in Rajaramnagar, near Islampur, in Sangli district, Maharashtra. | **Right.** The official website lists the institute at Rajaramnagar, Tal. Walwa, Dist. Sangli. |
| 3 | Which university is it affiliated with? | It is affiliated with Shivaji University, Kolhapur. | **Right.** The institute's official document identifies Shivaji University, Kolhapur as its affiliating university. |

Source used for checking: the institute's official website (ritindia.edu).

## My Notes

ChatGPT gave confident and correct answers to all three questions. The answers were specific and easy to understand, but I still checked them against the college's official information instead of assuming they were correct. All three being right does not mean it is always right, so for anything important, such as admission or fee details, I will verify it on the official site first.
